The logo loads, then moves into the homepage

Each option is a small preview of the homepage. The logo plays as the loading screen, then travels to its place in the hero section while the page builds around it. Click a preview to replay it.

The homepage text, buttons and marble background are placeholders to show the motion. On the real site the loading part waits until the 3D showroom and images have loaded, so it can be shorter or longer than shown here.
